# Datasets & Splits

## Dataset types
### A) Synthetic (required for Phase-1)
Generated by `01_simulator`.
- IID dataset: independent spectra samples
- Sequence dataset: time-ordered sequences with flicker/degradation/recovery patterns
- Stress datasets: distribution shifts (noise/illumination/humidity/distance/angle/mixtures)

### B) Real measurements (optional but strongly recommended)
Small curated set collected under controlled conditions:
- benign background sequences (no simulated hazard material)
- known safe proxies (non-hazard materials that produce structured spectra)
- if permitted, recorded detector outputs from training equipment / test ranges

Real measurements are used for:
- sanity check of indicator distributions,
- calibration of band definitions,
- qualitative validity of persistence behavior.

## Canonical dataset schema
### Sample-level (IID)
- sample_id
- wavelengths
- spectrum
- label (hazard|benign)
- latents_json (synthetic only)
- timestamps optional

### Sequence-level (time series)
- sequence_id
- t or timestamp
- sample_id (optional)
- spectrum (or x/z already computed)
- label (optional)
- scenario_tag (e.g., nominal, flicker, degradation, recovery)

## Splitting strategy (must be deterministic)
### Default split: hash split
- Split by `sequence_id` for time series (never split within a sequence)
- Split by `sample_id` for IID
- `train_frac=0.8`, `val_frac=0.1`, `test_frac=0.1`
- Hash function: SHA256(sequence_id) mod 1000 for bucket assignment
- Persist a `split_manifest.json` listing IDs in each split (or a hash + seed)

## Stress test sets
Stress sets must be disjoint from training sets by construction.
Define stress sets by ranges of latent variables not seen in training:
- higher noise sigma, different illumination temperature range,
- stronger baseline drift, more mixtures,
- heavier clipping probability,
- adverse humidity/angle/distance/angle extremes.

Store stress-set definitions (ranges + seed) in `experiments/configs/stress_*.yaml`.

## Leakage rules
- No test sequences or stress sequences may influence indicator band selection after freeze.
- Hyperparameter tuning must use train/val only.
- Reporting must clearly separate: tuned (val) vs final (test) metrics.
